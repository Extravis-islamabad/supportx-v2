**Erasure notes (S2b-1 follow-up, agreed with lane B):** `NewDeviceSignedIn` and
`AccountSecurityChanged` carry the device code and the approximate place in their outbox payloads,
never the address. The place is the city and country, or a site ID. Erasure deletes the person's
known devices and notice marks at once. A published outbox row keeps its payload until the relay's
purge, after `outbox_retention_hours` (72 by default), and consumers keep no copy. A delivered
notice keeps only its rendered text in the person's own inbox, and erasure handles that with their
notifications.

**Not done here:** naming the mapped site in the notice text. The notice says "a mapped site" (as
`sign_in_place.site_unknown` does). Naming it needs a public site-name lookup in `organization`,
which another lane owns (rule 8), so it is raised there instead of written here.
