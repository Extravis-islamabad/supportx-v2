### 2026-11-13 — WP-057 S1b (lane C): SSO required, break-glass, MFA with SSO, the Keycloak events import

- **Scope:** the second half of WP-057 session 1 (migration 0153, revising S1a's 0151; it is
  re-pointed onto main's head at the queue turn). Lane B blocks; its S1a/S1b conditions are the
  checklist.
- **Built:**
  - **Single sign-on required** (REQ-SEC-009): off, technicians or everyone.
    - It is judged from the verified token's method, before anyone is provisioned, at sign-in
      and at a tenant switch.
    - Turning it on needs an active break-glass admin, and the last one cannot go while it is on.
    - Break-glass admins are named by another tenant admin, need MFA, and get an optional,
      separately audited IP exemption.
    - Each emergency sign-in is audited, rate-limited per address and alerts the other admins.
  - **MFA with SSO** (REQ-SEC-026):
    - Default off: the provider's MFA is trusted.
    - On: SupportX's MFA applies after SSO too, and the tenant's own identity providers get the
      `supportx-otp-after-broker` post-broker flow.
  - **The verified method:** tooling/keycloak_identity.py puts the `sx_idp` session-note mapper
    on both app clients and makes the post-broker flow. Identity reads `identity_provider` from
    it.
  - **The Keycloak events import** (REQ-AUD-015 AC1), every 60 s as `supportx-events`
    (view-events only):
    - It reads error events from console and portal.
    - Attribution: by the provider alias, confirmed against the tenant's own providers, else by
      the redirect host; anything else is counted in a metric.
    - The cursor is a millisecond plus an offset, over settled milliseconds only. Writes are
      idempotent, and the cursor moves after the commit.
  - **Deploy:** `keycloak-events-client-secret` goes in the app Secret (secrets.sh, new and
    existing installs), passed only to the consumers worker and the identity Job.
  - **Screen:** the Single sign-on required card, with emergency administrators (en/ar/ur).
- **Agreed with lane B:**
  - attribution by redirect host or provider alias (Keycloak's events carry no organization);
  - no separate mobile client;
  - holders of the events secret;
  - paging inside a millisecond (not stepping past it);
  - the settle window (dateTo = now - 5 s);
  - the LDAP no-claim case in their federation test (rule 8 acknowledged).
- **Verified against Keycloak 26.8 (read-only):** the events API takes millisecond `dateFrom` and
  `dateTo` (both inclusive) and `direction=asc`.
