### 2026-11-11 — D-157: technicians on their tickets' assets, money behind its right, straight line by default, mail summary (lane B)

- **Technician's narrower write (D-157 item 1, completes D-153 item 2):** no new permission. The
  assets service's `writable` gives the full write to `assets.manage` on the asset; otherwise a
  person who sees the asset gets the narrower path when `ticket_access.ticket_grants` says so:
  the asset is linked to a ticket assigned to them that is open, paused or resolved-not-closed,
  and its holder is the ticket's requester or the requester's department. PATCH accepts only
  `location`; a life cycle move; fitting a free part into it or taking one out (the part's
  holder route). Anything else: 403 `asset_field_not_allowed` with `params.field`
  (en/ar/ur). History and audit as usual, under the technician. `GET /assets/{id}/access`
  (`manage`, `through_ticket`) drives the console.
