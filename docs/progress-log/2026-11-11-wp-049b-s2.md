- **REQ-AST-037 depreciation (pure domain, `assets/domain/financials.py`):** straight line,
  declining balance (150 %), double declining balance (200 %), sum of the years' digits, or none.
  Yearly amounts to the cent, the last year taking the rounding; the declining methods switch to
  straight line when that writes off more (so salvage is reached at the end of life); prorated by
  month (a month counts once its day comes, or the month's last day). Golden-value tests against
  the textbook 10,000 / 1,000 / 5-year example for each method (AC3); AC1 (1,200 over 3 years is
  800 after one) in the domain and the API; AC2 (the product's settings for assets without their
  own; `none` on the asset wins) in the API.
