  - **The pattern:** a step whose result comes later (an approval decided, tasks done, a function
    run, a call made) returns a pending result and waits, like a sub-flow. The owning side reports
    back in its own transaction through `automation.command_finished` (an outbox event), and
    automation's consumer continues the node. A result for a command no token waits on changes
    nothing.
  - **Approval node:** a level on the record on the WP-030 engine, with D-146's chair on a tie.
    `decide()` routes workflow rows to `workflow_approvals`.
  - **Task node:** a checklist on the record; `parts.workflow_task_settled` reports all done, or
    canceled or removed.
  - **Function node:** a worker runs it through `FunctionRunner` (§6.7), gated by the entitlement
    interface.
  - **Webhook node:** `app/shared/egress` from a worker; `Idempotency-Key: <instance>:<command>`.
  - **Notify node:** `work_items.workflow.notice` → `incident.workflow_notice`, with recipients
