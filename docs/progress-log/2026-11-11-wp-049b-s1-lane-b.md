### 2026-11-11 — WP-049b session 1: consumable stock, minimum stock, bookings ahead, loan leftovers (lane B)

On branch feat/wp-049b-s1, fresh from main. Migration **0130** (coordinator), down revision 0127
for now: it gets re-pointed to main's head before it queues. Design decisions from the
coordinator (2026-11-11): a consumable is a product of a consumable-kind type (D-141); stock per
(product, site) with a conditional decrement; an append-only ledger; minimum-stock alerts once and
re-armed above; bookings of a specific asset with a row lock and an overlap check (no
btree_gist); portal booking behind a tenant setting; the draft purchase request as a port for
WP-054.

- **Migration 0130 (expand only):**
  - six tables, each with tenant_id first in every index and RLS forced: `asset_consumables`,
    `asset_consumable_stocks` (quantity CHECK >= 0, one row per consumable and site with NULLS
    NOT DISTINCT, `low_alerted_at`), `asset_consumable_batches`, `asset_consumable_movements`
    (the ledger: the app role gets SELECT and INSERT only), `asset_stock_rules`,
    `asset_bookings`;
  - `asset_loans`: `display_id`, `department_id`, `site_id`, `booking_id`;
    `asset_settings.portal_booking` (default off);
  - the downgrade refuses while any tenant has a row in a new table, a loan using a new column
    or portal booking on (per tenant with app.tenant_id set; rule 5h).
- **REQ-AST-033 consumable stock:**
  - AC1: a receipt raises only that site's row;
  - AC2: lots (lot number, reference, made and expiry dates), an expired lot flagged;
  - AC3: switching batch tracking is refused (`asset_consumable_batch_tracking_fixed`, with the
    reason as its label).
- **REQ-AST-034 allocations:**
  - AC1: to a person, a department or an asset; more than there is is refused with
    `asset_stock_short` and the amount available;
  - AC2: `work_item_id` kept on the ledger line, `GET /assets/consumable-allocations?work_item_id=`
    for the request's side, the `assets.stock.moved` event names it; the console opens an
    allocation for a request with `/assets/consumables/:id?allocate_for=<id>`;
  - AC3: one `UPDATE ... SET quantity = quantity + n WHERE quantity + n >= 0 RETURNING`; the race
    test runs two allocations of the last unit at once: one 201, one 409 `asset_stock_ran_out`,
    one ledger line, stock 0.
- **REQ-AST-035 minimum stock:**
  - rules per product type or product and per site or every site; the most specific wins
    (domain `pick_rule`);
  - AC1: 6 → 4 with a minimum of 5 sends one `assets.stock.low`; → 3 none; back above 5 re-arms;
    the next fall alerts again (tested); new notification event `asset.stock_low` to the rule's
    people (`stock_watchers`), with quantity and minimum;
  - AC2: the `DraftPurchaseRequests` port (assets' public interface) is called on an alert whose
    rule asks for one; procurement registers it with WP-054. Until then none is made (logged).
    Tested with a recording port.
- **REQ-AST-032 bookings ahead:**
  - AC1: a specific loanable asset; the asset row is locked, then the period is checked against
    its live bookings and its open loan; a clash is refused with `asset_booking_taken` and
    `next_free` (same length, whole days later); a race test of two bookings at once; a loan
    over a booked time is refused (`asset_booked`);
  - AC2: hand over makes a loan with the booking's dates, through the loan service (every loan
    rule holds), and marks the booking handed over;
  - AC3: `GET/POST /assets/my-bookings`, `GET /assets/bookable`, cancel own, only while
    `portal_booking` is on (`asset_portal_booking_off` otherwise).
- **REQ-AST-030 leftovers:** LN-n display IDs on new loans; a department borrower (the
  department holds the assets, the borrower collects them and hears the notices); the loan's
  site; `GET /assets/loans?status=open|overdue|closed&site_id=`.
- **Events and channels:** `assets.consumable.changed`, `assets.stock.moved`,
