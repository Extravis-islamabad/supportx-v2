## Done

- **Checklists (REQ-INC-040, -077, -078).**
  - Migration 0144: `work_item_checklists`, `work_item_checklist_items`, `checklist_library` and
    `work_item_reminders`, each with tenant RLS and an RLS test; `checklist_item_max` (1..1000,
    default 100) and template checklists.
  - Typed or library checklists, several per record. A tick records who and when; empty lines
    are left out and counted.
  - Unticked items are open work (OpenWorkKind.CHECKLISTS), completed by the system on close.
  - Record templates add their checklists when their condition matches.
  - The library: CRUD, plus a CSV import that only reads (5 MB, 5000 rows, 40 columns; bad rows
    listed by row and reason).
  - Library changes emit `WorkItemSettingsChanged` so screens update live.
- **Item limit:** `checklist_item_max` in Admin › Incidents › Closing. A list past the limit is
  refused whole (`too_many_items` with its max), never cut.
- **All files (REQ-INC-080).**
  - The "All files" tab lists the record's own files and those on its replies and notes.
  - Pick any number of openable ones and download them as one zip:
    `POST /files/records/{type}/{id}/archive`.
  - Lane B's rules:
    - the record must be visible first, else 404 with no file name;
    - each file is checked again;
    - files left out are listed in `manifest.txt` by ID and reason only;
    - data keys are unwrapped before streaming and no DB session is held while it streams;
    - it fails closed mid-stream;
    - at most 2 downloads per person at once (Valkey sorted set, 429 `archive_busy`);
    - limits of 200 files and 500 MB;
    - audited as `file.archive_downloaded` (DATA).
- **Personal reminders (REQ-INC-082).**
  - On the record (a side card) and in the header (a clock with a due badge, and a drawer with
    every open reminder, soonest first).
  - Only the owner's, administrators included. They change live on the personal channel only.
  - A due sweep alerts once (the `alerted_at` claim); a record the owner can no longer see shows
    "no longer available", with no alert sent.
- Labels in en, ar and ur, with Arabic's six plural forms on every counted label.
