### 2026-11-18 — WP-057 S4d: the security overview (lane C)

- **The API:** `GET /identity/security-overview` (`identity.manage`, the identity rate limit,
  `no-store`). Each tile comes from the function its own screen uses, so the numbers always agree
  (lane B's condition 2):
  - MFA from `mfa.list_policies`; single sign-on from `get_sso_policy` and the expiry notices'
    `expiry_items`; passwords and sessions from the identity settings; network from the rules;
  - keys through `key_state`, which `GET /api-keys` now uses too (moved out of the router);
  - service accounts from `list_service_accounts`; failed and locked sign-ins through
    `count_events`, which shares the log's filter builder with `list_events`;
  - sensitive fields by seal state from metadata's `seal_counts`, registered with identity_access
    at load (no import cycle), and the data key's date (`fields.key_ring_since`).
- **Data region:** the instance setting `SUPPORTX_DATA_REGION`, read only; blank hides the tile.
- **Flags:** codes and counts only, most severe first, with the recommendations they compare to.
  Nothing is enforced. The thresholds are in #537's description for the product owner.
- **The console:** "Security at a glance" at the top of the page: KPI tiles that open their card or
  page, and the flags in words with a link each. Live on the identity and people channels.
- **Also:** `GET /api-keys/policy-impact` names `api_keys.manage` at the route; the
  service-accounts setup text says they act through API keys.
