### 2026-11-11 — WP-045d S3: change notifications, reminders and change SLAs (lane A)

No migration. One new event: `work_items.change.reminder_due`.

- **Notifications (REQ-CHG-103, -104):**
  - `ChangeNotifications` (record type change) carries the Appendix A defaults: submitted,
    approval needed (and nobody to decide), approved, rejected, clarification, assigned,
    implemented, failed, backed out, closed, sent back, override used, board with no members,
    mentions and notes;
  - it replaces the reply-only change source (#359) and delegates replies to it;
  - "entered a status" (off by default) takes a `status` condition, chosen on the rules screen;
  - role holders are resolved when the event is sent, at most 200 per kind.
- **Access (lane B's design review):**
  - staff who see the change get it in full;
  - its requester gets the public side only (number, title, status);
  - someone with a live approval on it gets the public side and their own one-time approval
    link, so an approver without `changes.view` is still told. A decided approval gives
    nothing;
  - public notices link to the portal home until the portal has a change page (S4).
- **Reminders (REQ-CHG-091, -105):**
  - a module schedule every 5 minutes covers: the window starts within the hour, the window
    ended without implementation, a CAB member still to decide on a change starting within a
    day, and the review date reached;
  - each is claimed once in `processed_events` on a key of the change, the kind, the time and
    the member, with look-backs inside the claims' retention;
  - at send time the change is read again: a moved window's old reminder says nothing, a moved
    window gets a fresh one, and cancelled or closed changes are never reminded.
- **Change SLAs (REQ-CHG-107):**
  - changes are an SLA type with no default targets, so nothing is seeded;
  - an admin's policy targets start clocks, escalations, the trash rule and the safety net, as
    for the other types;
  - the change header shows its due time;
  - the SLA policies tab offers incidents, requests, problems and changes, and says when a type
    has no targets.
  - Business rules on changes work (tested). REQ-CHG-106 is lane C's Workflows tab (#379).
- **Open:**
  - the health-check default waits for health checks (REQ-CHG-086);
  - the testing owner is not a change role;
  - the SLA settings tab is still incidents only.
- **Tests:**
  - DB: `test_change_notifications.py` (10) and `test_change_sla.py` (2);
  - regressions: notifications, replies, approvals, problems and changes, 82 passed; SLA
    policies, clock, timers and safety net with changes, 80 passed;
  - console: the notification rules (status picker) and SLA policies (type choice) tests pass;
  - the label checks pass.
