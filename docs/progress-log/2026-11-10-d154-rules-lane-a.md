# 2026-11-10 — D-154 "rules": escalations and automation follow the group (lane A)

- **SLA:** `Action.group_id` on escalation actions. The level editor's checks
  (`reassign_group_required`, `person_not_in_group`, `person_in_no_group`) fill it in for a
  person in one group. The API in and out carries it.
- **work_items:** `rule_assignment.place_person` is the one run-time helper for escalations,
  triggers and business rules. With the person active, not Away and in the group, the record
  moves to the group and to them. Otherwise it goes to the group only, the automation ref says
  why (`unavailable`, `person_id`), and the event `work_items.work_item.assignee_unavailable`
  is written.
- **Notice:** `incident.assignee_unavailable` (in-app) goes to the step's owner, else to
  holders of `sla.manage` / `automation.manage`.
- **automation:** `away_user_ids` and `step_owner` are public. At save, a rule or trigger
  setting a technician must say the group when the person is in several (never part of
  `check_values`, so a run never refuses).
- **Migration 0126:** fills the only group into saved levels, per tenant; config rows only;
  counts logged; the downgrade has nothing to undo.
- **Lane B's #339 point:** a ready request tells its group once per completed required task,
  never on an optional tick.
- **Labels:** en/ar/ur for the event, the notice, the field errors and the history's why-line.
- **Tests:** `tests/db/test_d154_rules.py` (11 cases: editor, escalation move/away/left/
  inactive, rule move/away with the owner's notice, rule and trigger save, trigger move, the
  0126 data step), `test_escalation_groups_migration.py`, `test_fulfilment_api.py` E1, and
  `automationWhy.test.tsx`.
- **Not in this PR:** a person picker for "reassign to a person" in the SLA level editor
  (escalations to a person are API-only today; the console offers groups), and the notice for
  service requests, problems and changes (rules run on incidents first; the incident source
  carries the notice).
