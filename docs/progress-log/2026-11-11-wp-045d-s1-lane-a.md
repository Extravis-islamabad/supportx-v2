- **Tasks per stage (REQ-CHG-088, -094):**
  - a staged record's task has a stage on its path, by default the one the record is in;
  - leaving a stage with its own open tasks is refused (`stage_tasks_open`, labelled). That
    includes the system move an approval makes: the approval says why;
  - cancelling is never held up.
- **The review (REQ-CHG-090, -091):**
  - the change's plan has the review's sections (findings, went well, went badly). They are
    never watched for re-approval and never copied;
  - the review's outcome is the review stage's;
  - `review_due_at` is on the change's details. Its reminder comes with S3's notices.
- **Child changes and releases (REQ-CHG-096):**
  - `/changes/{id}/children` lists, adds and removes them: one parent each, never itself, no
    loops (`change_would_loop`);
  - closing waits for every child (`children_open`). Cancelling does not.
- **Closing (REQ-CHG-092, -095):** the closure code, and a closing description saved as the
  record's outcome text.
- **Closed is locked (REQ-CHG-097):** a closed or cancelled change refuses edits, details, plans,
  tasks, worklogs, its schedule and its downtimes with `record_closed` (labelled). Audit, the
  lifecycle sink and history still write.
