### 2026-11-11 — WP-064 session 2: fingerprints make record history tamper-evident; erasure (lane B)

- **Chain format (ADR-046, migration 0129):**
  - a lifecycle entry gains `fingerprint` (`fp1:<hex>`), `fingerprint_saves` (the change IDs it
    covers) and `personal_subjects` (whose salts it uses), hashed only when set: every older
    entry hashes as before, and exports carry them without a verifier change;
  - the entry holds digests of the values in `field_changes`, never the values;
  - the canonical encoding is written out in ADR-046 (values, numbers, strings, order, salts,
    prefix) and pinned by a golden vector:
    `fp1:f27b292b6b9a3063d3ee32b318c7215b3aa4df90693a55d1dd3c230f7d1ea66d`.
- **Personal fields** (`audit/domain/lifecycle.py`, one block for lanes A and C to review):
  work items' free text (subject, description, resolution, reason text, custom values, task
  titles; problems' analysis; changes' and releases' plans, outcome notes, downtime text),
  whose subjects are the requester and the on-behalf person (work_items registers a subject
  resolver in `app/shared/history`); assets none; people later, when they are a lifecycle type.
- **Salts:** `audit_subject_salts`, RLS forced, no app rights at all; reached only through the
  migrator-owned definers `audit_subject_salts_ensure`, `audit_subject_salts_of` and the new
  `audit_erase_subject(subject, history_rows)`. Erasure keeps a tombstone, so an erased person
  never gets a fresh salt.
- **Verifier:** per entry `verified`, `mismatch` or `erased_on_request` (only with a later
  `audit.subject_erased` entry); counts on each run, `fingerprint_mismatch` fails the run; the
  entry view returns `fingerprint_status`. Labels en/ar/ur.
- **Erasure:** blanks the person's free text in history (rows of saves listing them only),
  deletes their personal rows, destroys the salt; everyone else still verifies.
- **Tests:** golden vector and encoding (unit); save, tamper (changed and removed rows),
  erasure with and without the logged entry, other subjects after an erasure, older entries,
  no app access to salts, per-tenant salts, the definer's row check, the downgrade refusal and
  the down/up run with the sink off (DB). The audit, history, work-item and asset suites pass.
- **Not done here:** the console mark (S4); people records as a lifecycle type; partial
  verification of an erased entry's ordinary fields (one fingerprint needs every salt).
