### 2026-11-11 — WP-064 session 2: fingerprints make record history tamper-evident; erasure (lane B)

- **Chain format (ADR-046, migration 0129):**
  - a lifecycle entry gains two commitments, hashed only when set: `fingerprint` over the
    ordinary fields (no salt) and `personal_fingerprint` over the personal fields (salted,
    only when there are any), with `fingerprint_saves` (the change IDs) and
    `personal_subjects` (whose salts). Every older entry hashes as before, and exports carry
    them without a verifier change;
  - why two (lane B's review): with one salted fingerprint, changing an ordinary value and
    then erasing the subject would read "erased on request". The ordinary half has no salt, so
    it still reads "mismatch";
  - the entry holds digests of the values in `field_changes`, never the values;
  - the canonical encoding is written out in ADR-046 (values, numbers, strings, order, salts,
    halves, reasons, prefix) and pinned by golden vectors: A (an asset, ordinary only, with a
    reason) `fp1:1257eee2a95aea2d289191c3ce95e9199cf4db92bd3aa5ee7c5dc4217d6065d5`; B ordinary
    `fp1:929257682249c7d55f6eebe8a2e94f6c22d53980a5de35d75c7c6e1aa8bc631a`, personal (with a
    reason) `fp1:c2f4a08e9f972dd8d74b5852cafe7f65a0ee0aac2c3be28953983f2b114afa10`;
  - every row's free-text reason (`field_changes.reason`) is committed to: salted in the
    personal half for work items, unsalted in the ordinary half for assets (lanes A and C);
- **Personal fields** (`audit/domain/lifecycle.py`, one block for lanes A and C to review):
  work items' free text (subject, description, resolution, tags, custom values, task titles;
  problems' analysis; changes' and releases' plans, outcome notes, downtime text; changes'
  risk reason; releases' notes, version and build reference) and every save's reason, whose
  subjects are the requester and the on-behalf person, plus the previous one when a save
  replaces them (work_items registers a subject
  resolver in `app/shared/history`); assets none; people later, when they are a lifecycle type.
- **Salts:** `audit_subject_salts`, RLS forced, no app rights at all; reached only through the
  migrator-owned definers `audit_subject_salts_ensure`, `audit_subject_salts_of` and the new
  `audit_erase_subject(subject, history_rows)`. Erasure keeps a tombstone, so an erased person
  never gets a fresh salt.
- **Verifier:** per entry `verified`, `mismatch` (always when the ordinary half differs) or
  `erased_on_request` (the ordinary half matches, and the salt is gone with a later
  `audit.subject_erased` entry); counts on each run, `fingerprint_mismatch` fails the run; the
  entry view returns `fingerprint_status`. Labels en/ar/ur.
- **Erasure:** blanks the person's free text and the reasons in history (rows of saves listing
  them only),
  deletes their personal rows, destroys the salt; everyone else still verifies.
- **Tests:** golden vectors and encoding (unit); save, tamper (changed and removed rows), an
  ordinary value changed before an erasure (still a mismatch), an ordinary row blanked through
  the definer (a mismatch), a changed reason, an erased reason, an asset's ordinary reason, the
  previous requester,
  erasure with and without the logged entry, other subjects after an erasure, older entries,
  no app access to salts, per-tenant salts, the definer's row check, the downgrade refusal and
  the down/up run with the sink off (DB). The audit, history, work-item and asset suites pass.
- **Not done here:** the console mark (S4); people records as a lifecycle type.
