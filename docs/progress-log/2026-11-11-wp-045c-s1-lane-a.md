### 2026-11-11 — WP-045c S1: change windows, conflicts and the freeze override (lane A)

The coordinator approved the plan: migration **0132**, **ADR-042**, D-156 held (defaults: a freeze
blocks, a maintenance window warns, weekly repeat only).

- **Domain (`domain/change_windows.py`, pure):**
  - windows, scopes and weekly occurrences;
  - conflicts: a freeze (blocks or warns by its mode), outside every maintenance window in scope
    (a warning), and an overlap with another open change in the same group or at the same site
    (a warning);
  - a window's own checks.
- **Migration 0132:**
  - `change_windows` (RLS, actor audit), with a downgrade guard that stops while a tenant keeps a
    window or an override;
  - `work_item_change_details.freeze_override_reason`, `_by` and `_at`.
- **Services:**
  - `change_conflicts` reads windows and the other changes' implementation windows, and never
    imports the work item service. Its `guard` runs on every save of a change's implementation
    window or planned downtime.
  - `change_windows` covers create, update and remove (manage permission, If-Match, history,
    outbox event), the conflicts view, and the freeze override at the change's scope (reason,
    history, new record version).
- **API:**
  - `/api/v1/change-windows` (with an optional range for the calendar);
  - `GET /changes/{id}/conflicts`;
  - `POST /changes/{id}/freeze-override`, audited with `change.freeze_override`, refused attempts
    included.
- **Permissions:**
  - `changes.manage_windows` (all) and `changes.override_freeze` (all, site, group), both granted
    to the change manager;
  - administrators hold every permission; team leads and technicians hold neither (role matrix
    and its snapshot updated);
  - the service desk manager's refused list includes both.
- **Labels** in en, ar and ur: permissions, audit actions and object, errors and field codes,
  enums (`change_window_kind`, `change_window_mode`, `change_conflict`), the event type, and the
  `freeze_override` history field.
- **Fixed while testing:** "outside maintenance" read only the windows overlapping the change, so
  it never saw a window the change was outside of. A one-off maintenance window now counts within
  a week of the change, and a weekly one while it repeats (ADR-042, `MAINTENANCE_REACH`).
- **Tests:**
  - `test_change_windows_domain.py` (7);
  - `test_change_windows_api.py` (9): defaults, the technician read-only, RLS, checks, the guard
    on the window and downtimes, weekly freezes, conflicts, the override at scope with audit and
    history, no permission;
  - `test_change_windows_migration.py`.
