### 2026-11-11 — WP-046a S1, PR 2: task notifications, change bulk cancel and trash (lane A)

No migration; 0141 (PR 1) holds the wider checks. Two new events:
`work_items.task.assigned` and `work_items.task.reminder_due`.

- **Task notifications (REQ-INC-073 AC2, -076 AC1):**
  - one record source, `task`, for every type. Its owner hears that a task is given to them
    (required), that its scheduled start is near (its own reminder), and that it is overdue;
  - who may see the record gets the full notice with its link; a task-only owner (D-112) gets
    the public side with a link to My tasks; anyone else gets nothing;
  - a five-minute sweep claims each reminder once in `processed_events` (consumer
    `task_reminders`). The source re-reads the task at send time, so a moved time, a new owner
    or a done task says nothing;
  - default texts in en/ar/ur.
- **Change bulk (REQ-CHG-101):**
  - cancel with one reason, through each change's own listed cancel move; a change past the
    point a cancel is offered is skipped and says so;
  - move to trash through the same remove as one record;
  - incidents never offer either (422);
  - on the change list's bulk bar, with a dialog (the reason required for cancel).
- **Loops:** add_child refuses a child that already holds the change, through
  sub_release_of, part_of_change or in_release. It uses lane C's `release_links.holds`, now
  public at their ask.
