  - **Recent re-authentication** (REQ-SEC-027):
    - `RecentAuth` sits on 28 sensitive routes, listed in `RECENT_AUTH_ROUTES` and checked both
      ways.
    - A 10-minute window measured from `auth_time`. The resolver records the session's
      freshness on the request; API keys and the mobile app are refused with their own codes.
    - `/auth/confirm` sends the browser to Keycloak with `prompt=login` and `max_age=0` (plus

    - Refusals go in the sign-in log as `reauth_failed`.
  - **The pop-up:** the dialog asks first, and its click opens the pop-up. `/confirmed` (console
    and portal) answers on a same-origin BroadcastChannel tagged with the opener's attempt id,
    and the api-client retries the refused change once.

    only), and "Sign out everywhere else".
