## Done

- **Costs (REQ-INC-068).**
  - Migration 0147: worklogs gain `billable`, `cost_rate`, `technician_cost` and `draft`;
    `work_item_worklog_costs` and `work_item_timers` are new, each with tenant RLS and a test;
    three closure settings.
  - The cost rate comes from identity_access's `cost_rate` (#427) through work_items' port, as a
    snapshot taken when the time started, and is taken again when the owner, start or duration
    changes. No rate gives no cost, never a zero.
  - Other-cost lines: the base currency, or another currency with the rate paid at; a line's
    problems name it (`costs.<n>.<field>`).
  - `<type>.view_costs` (D-164) hides every figure without it: `money_hidden` in the API; the
    console offers no cost entry then.
- **Closed records (REQ-INC-070).** With "No time on closed incidents" on, a closed record refuses
  new, changed and removed time (409 `worklog_record_closed`). The switches use the Closing tab's
  per-type wording (releases say "release").
- **Timers (REQ-INC-069).** Start, stop (the worklog form opens prefilled; nothing is logged
  until saved), discard; `/my-timers`. Resolving, closing or canceling saves running timers as
  drafts with their cost; a draft counts once its owner confirms it. Auto-start (off by default)
  starts once for the record's technician. `part.changed` v3's `timer` part goes to the owner's
  personal channel only.
