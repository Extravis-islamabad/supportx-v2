### 2026-11-13 — WP-046a S5b: form designer and form rules (lane A)

The coordinator approved the plan in the S5 plan (S5a → S5b → S5c) and gave migration 0155.
Lane C reviews the designer and the create form; lane B the portal filtering and server
enforcement.

## Done

- **Form rules (REQ-INC-037).**
  - `domain/form_rules.py` and its TypeScript twin, `packages/forms/src/rules/evaluate.ts`.
  - Conditions are a bounded part of the filter DSL: eq, ne, in, not_in, is_null and
    is_not_null; and/or at most 3 deep; at most 20 conditions.
  - Values compare as text and IDs ignore case. A missing value matches ne, not_in and is_null
    only. Lists mean "includes".
  - Actions are show, hide (with keep_value), require, lock and set. Evaluation is one pass in
    rule order over the values as typed, and a later rule wins.
  - Shared vectors in `docs/architecture/form-rule-vectors.json` (11 cases plus 9 invalid ones)
    run in pytest and Vitest (AC3).
- **Enforcement on the server.** `templates.apply_form_rules` runs before the usual checks on
  create and edit:
  - a rule's value fills an empty field;
  - a hidden field is cleared unless kept, and the clear goes through history;
  - a rule's `required` is the same field error as the browser's (AC1); on an edit only when
    the field is newly required or emptied (D-086);
  - a locked field changed by hand is `field_locked`.
- **Layouts (REQ-INC-036).**
  - `domain/form_layout.py` and the `arrangeFields` twin: sections, columns, collapsible, label
    position, and a requester layout of its own or the technician one.
  - A field no section names goes at the end of the last section.
- **Migration 0155:** `layout` and `form_rules` on `work_item_templates`, with shape checks.
  The downgrade is refused once either is used (rule 5h); a DB test proves it.
- **API.**
  - Template create, copy and patch take and return a typed `layout` and `form_rules`, checked
    against the tenant's fields.
  - New error codes, labelled: `not_a_form_field`, `rule_too_deep`, `field_locked`,
    `needs_collapsible`.
  - The portal template carries its sections and only the rules over the requester's own
    fields (036 AC2).
- **Console.**
  - The create form follows the layout and applies the rules live; a locked field is shown
    disabled. The classic form is unchanged without a layout.
  - The template editor has the designer (keyboard moves; preview of the technician and
    requester forms, left to right and right to left, 036 AC3; a new-custom-field link) and
    the rule editor (the shared filter builder over the form's choice fields).
- **Portal:** the report form applies the safe rules.
- Labels in en, ar and ur for every new string.
