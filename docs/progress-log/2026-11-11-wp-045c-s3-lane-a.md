### 2026-11-11 — WP-045c S3: the change calendar and the windows admin (lane A)

No migration. Builds on S1 (#376) and S2.

- **Calendar read:** `GET /api/v1/change-calendar?start&end&mine` returns the changes you may see
  (the changes list's visibility) planned in [start, end): implementation windows and planned
  downtimes. Each comes with its own version for If-Match. The range is at most 400 days. It has
  its own prefix, because `/changes/calendar` would meet the changes' `/{record_id}` route first.
- **Calendar page (`/changes/calendar`, linked from the changes list):**
  - month, week, day and agenda views, with "only mine" and the freeze and maintenance layers;
  - days are wall-clock days in the person's zone, and the week starts on the preferred day;
  - weekday and month names come from `Intl` in the person's language;
  - live via the changes' list channel and the windows' broadcast.
- **Drag to reschedule:** drag a change's window to another day. It keeps its time of day,
  including across summer time; the re-approval warning shows first; it sends the window's
  If-Match; a refusal (for example a blocking freeze) links to the change.
- **Date arithmetic (`calendarMath.ts`):** pure, with its own tests: grids, zone days, ranges,
  weekly occurrences as the server reckons them, and day shifts.
- **Windows admin (`/admin/change-windows`, template E):**
  - the list with kind, mode, when (weekly until) and where;
  - add, edit and delete through RowActions and EditDrawer. A new window's kind sets its default
    mode (D-156, held);
  - `changes.manage_windows` or `settings.manage`; live.
- **Also:**
  - the repeat-rules settings wait for the plan before fetching the rules. That was lane B's
    flake report: `has()` answers yes while the plan loads;
  - labels in en, ar and ur.
- **Tests:**

  - `calendar.test.tsx` (3): month layers, drag with If-Match, agenda;
  - `windows.test.tsx` (2).
