# D-104 (2026-10-28)

## Decision

**WP-039 session 3 defaults** (built under D-089 D5, D7 and the product owner's lock-out direction in D-103). **Product owner review (2026-10-28): approved as listed; WP-039 approved.** (1) **Lock-out:** Keycloak 26.8.1 cannot double the wait (measured: its strategies are equal steps), so the realm uses LINEAR: 5 failures, then **15 minutes, 15 more for each further failure, at most 1 hour** (a cap, so a stranger who knows an address cannot keep its owner out for a day); failures are remembered 12 hours (a 15-minute window would reset the count as each lock ends, so it would never grow). Moved only from the shipped old values; an operator's own values are kept. (2) **One message for every failed sign-in** (wrong password, locked, unknown address) in en/ar/ur: "Invalid username or password. After several failed attempts, sign-in pauses: wait at least 15 minutes, then try again." It never reveals whether an account exists or is locked (verified: a locked account with the right password reads exactly like an unknown address). (3) **MFA policy per role** with Appendix A defaults (required: tenant admin, security admin, auditor, technician; optional: requester and custom roles); a person's mode is the strictest of their roles; the **grace period** (7 days, 0 to 30) runs from when MFA became required for that person (role or policy change). During it a reminder with a set-up button; after it Keycloak's own required actions (code generator, then recovery codes) are added, so the next sign-in enrols first; the sign-in itself checks too (the hourly schedule is a backstop) and never loops when Keycloak is unreachable (`mfa_enrolment_unavailable`). Required actions are only added, never removed: one Keycloak user serves all of a person's tenants and the strictest wins (D-089). (4) **Recovery codes** are Keycloak's (12, each once, asked in order); the deploy step turns the browser flow's recovery-code step from DISABLED to ALTERNATIVE once. (5) **MFA reset:** `identity.manage`, never one's own (`mfa_reset_own`); removes the authenticator and recovery codes; if MFA is required the person enrols at their next sign-in, without a new grace; audited (`user.mfa_reset`); the person gets an e-mail in their language through the mail foundation (no link, names no admin). (6) **My account** (console My profile, portal My account): password change, MFA set-up and new recovery codes happen on Keycloak's own pages, started from SupportX (application-initiated actions) with `prompt=login`, so the current password is always asked; recent sign-ins from Keycloak's login events (7 days). (7) **Idle timeout on screen:** the warning 2 minutes ahead counts the person's own activity (live updates make requests by themselves), and the screen signs out at the limit; typed input of the new-incident form is kept in the tab's session storage and restored after signing in again (`useKeptDraft`, PR #87, agreed with lane A; the record page and composer follow in lane A's chain). (8) **Security administrator** system role (Appendix A): sign-in and security settings, MFA policy and reset, people view; no record access.
