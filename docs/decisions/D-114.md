# D-114 (2026-10-28)

## Decision

**Dashboard widget library** (product owner request, lane C). "Add widget" opens a searchable catalogue grouped by module and section (key numbers, open work by …, queues and lists, trends, time, built-in reports, coming later): 43 curated widgets plus every built-in report as a chart or table. New engine parts: a **flow report** (`flow: {days, bucket}`: per day/week/month the records created, resolved, and the backlog at the bucket's end; the backlog is read from each record's own timestamps as they stand now, a cancellation counting from its last status change, not a history replay); **`@my_groups`** in report filters, resolved on the server from the signed-in session for every run (so "my groups' queue" cannot be widened from the browser; `@me` is now also resolved on the server); **records widgets** (`records: {entity, filter, sort, limit ≤ 20}`, a short list read through the entity's own list API in the viewer's scope; their filter may use `@me` but not `@my_groups`); new measures overdue, due today, awaiting first response and reopened. **SLA and approval widgets are shown in a "coming later" section with the package that brings them, greyed out, rather than hidden**: people comparing with ServiceDesk Plus see they are planned. The built-in Service desk (17 widgets) and My work (9) dashboards are placed from the catalogue. Every widget shows only what the viewer may see.

## Rationale

Product owner: "we have 6 widgets; ServiceDesk Plus has dozens".
