# D-052 (2026-10-24)

## Decision

**Technicians' built-in default scope is `group`** for incident view and work: they see incidents of their own groups, incidents assigned to them, and the **unassigned** incidents of their sites (the new-ticket queue). Admins can widen roles to `all`. The first-run setup must offer this choice (open item until a setup wizard exists).

## Rationale

Least privilege by default without hiding new tickets from everyone.
