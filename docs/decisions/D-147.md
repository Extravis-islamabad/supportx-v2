# D-147 (2026-11-09)

## Decision

**UI pass 3: one set of page templates for every screen** (the product owner, through the
coordinator, 2026-11-09, after reviewing the screenshots).

Global fixes:

1. The sidebar runs the full page height on long pages, while scrolling and in full-page
   screenshots.
2. Top bar:
   - the organisation name stays on one line, truncated, with a tooltip;
   - "Appearance" moves into the user menu;
   - every icon button has a tooltip.
3. Notices become one compact, dismissible strip: the most important notice, plus a "N more"
   link on the same line.
4. Pages use the available width, with no half-width cards and empty space beside them. Forms
   are at most about 960 px wide, centred or with a side help column; lists are full width.

Templates, as shared components in `packages/ui`; no module builds its own layout:

- **A. Record page** (incidents, service requests, problems, changes, releases, assets):
  - a compact header with the icon, ID, title, status, priority and SLA chips, and one line of
    key facts;
  - all actions on one row, Follow included;
  - tabs and content on the left, compact summary cards on the right;
  - Properties shows only the filled fields, with "Add details" for the rest. A click edits in
    place, and there is no pencil on every row;
  - no large empty void in either column.
- **B. Form page** (create and edit: announcements, catalogue items, rules, settings forms):
  - labels above the fields, with consistent spacing;
  - logical sections in cards;
  - help text once, under its field;
  - field widths that match their content;
  - one sticky footer bar with Cancel to the left of the primary action, never buttons in the
    middle of the form.
- **C. List page:**
  - the title and count, and one toolbar row: view, search, filters and columns;
  - at most two or three header buttons, the rest in "More";
  - the table fits its card at 1280 and 1440 with no overflow; the subject column takes the
    remaining width, and the others are sized to their content.
- **D. Library page** (reports, knowledge, the dashboards list):
  - a full-width grid or table grouped by module, with tabs or sections (incidents, service
    requests, problems, changes, assets);
  - search and favourites on top.
- **E. Settings page:** a left section menu, the page header, grouped form cards and one save bar
  (#258), on every settings page.

Specific fixes from the screenshots:

- **Incident record:**
  - Follow aligned with the other actions;
  - no empty void below the left column;
  - Properties without clutter;
  - no duplicate Subject;
  - the Worklogs form laid out properly;
  - one time-zone note, not two;
  - a disabled "Log time" says why.
- **Incidents list:**
  - no table overflow (QA9-V-01);
  - the subject width fixed;
  - fewer header buttons;
  - "My exports" not looking selected.
- **Service request record** on template A:
  - one "Awaiting approval" chip;
  - the requester shown;
  - the conversation;
  - the right panel (QA9-V-05).
- **New announcement** on template B.
- **Reports** on template D, with the reports of every module.

How:

- Lane C builds the global fixes and templates A-E, then migrates its own screens: shell,
  settings shell, reports, knowledge, dashboards, announcements, releases, AI and admin setup.
- Lane A migrates the incident, service request, problem and change records, lists and forms.
  Lane B migrates assets and the people and identity settings.
- One PR per piece, no stacked PRs. Each migrated screen has before and after screenshots at
  1440, 1280 and 390, in English and Arabic.
- Every template screen joins the visual matrix.
- From now on the daily QA checks each screen against its template.

## Rationale

The screens were built module by module and no longer look like one product: different headers,
half-empty pages, buttons in the middle of forms and tables wider than their cards. Shared
templates make the look consistent once, and checkable by QA, instead of fixing it screen by
screen.

## Affected docs

PROGRESS.md (UI pass 3 work package, its PR split and status), docs/architecture (the templates'
component guide in packages/ui), the visual matrix (template screens), the daily QA checklist.
