# D-032 (2026-10-23)

## Decision

**WP-012 walking skeleton = Announcements**, in a new module `announcements` (ADR-024), licensed with the `incident` feature. Banners in the console and the portal. Publishing may require a second person (four-eyes), a tenant setting **off by default**. Unpublish and archive take an optional reason, kept in the history. A scheduled publish window must go live on every screen with no reload. No scaffold generator: a checklist (`docs/architecture/module-template.md`).

## Rationale

One real but low-risk entity exercises every platform rule; the checklist is enough until more modules exist.
