# D-078 (2026-10-26)

## Decision

**SupportX branding:** the logo (docs/brand/supportx-logo-dark-text.png) and its generated versions (white text for dark mode, one colour for high contrast, icon, favicons) appear wherever SupportX shows its own brand. Colours sampled from the logo and adjusted for WCAG AA: **primary #7e40d1** (white text 5.93:1), **gradient #6060de → #951ab6** (white text at least 4.97:1); dark-mode links #bea0e8 (8.23:1 on #141414), high contrast #522a88 (10.27:1). They replace the earlier Extravis purple #9619B5. Logo and colours come from one place (`packages/ui/src/brand`) so WP-017 can replace them per tenant.

## Rationale

Product owner request (lane B); colours proposed by the session and applied as instructed.
