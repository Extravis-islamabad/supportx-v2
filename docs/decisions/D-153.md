# D-153 (2026-11-10)

## Decision

**The built-in roles are split and extended.** Haider, through the coordinator, 2026-11-10.

1. **The old combined service desk manager splits in two:**
   - **Team lead** (`team_lead`, new): the technician's work on its groups' incidents, requests and
     problems, capped at group scope (creating and approving stay as the technician's). It
     manages its own groups only (D-152) and views changes, assets and the CMDB without changing
     them.
   - **Service desk manager** (`service_desk_manager`, kept): every desk record and the reports,
     with no system settings (no `settings.manage`, `groups.manage` or `users.manage`).
     **It configures the desk, never the tenant** (Haider, 2026-11-10):
     - *Allowed:*
       - incident and request setup: categories, templates, reply templates, statuses (`config.service_desk`);
       - SLAs (`sla.manage`);
       - business rules and routing (`automation.manage`);
       - the desk's portal content and announcements (`config.announcements`, `announcements.manage`);
       - desk views and dashboards (`dashboards.create`, `dashboards.share`).
     - *Refused:*
       - sign-in and security (`identity.manage`, `api_keys.manage`);
       - integrations and mail (`integrations.manage_email`, `integrations.manage_identity`, `mailboxes.manage`);
       - people and their import (`users.manage`), and roles (`roles.manage`);
       - licence (`licence.manage`), branding (`branding.manage`) and organisation (`config.organisation`);
       - audit (`audit.manage`);
       - tenant settings (`settings.manage`);
       - **change advisory boards and the emergency override** (`changes.manage_boards`, `changes.emergency_override`; the change manager and administrators only, #327).
     The role-matrix test pins both lists.
   - **No role assigns outside a record's group** (D-154a): `*.assign_outside_group` no longer
     exists, so no built-in role holds it.
2. **Technician:** views assets and the CMDB, and updates only assets linked to its own tickets.
   Full asset management moves to the new **Asset manager** (`asset_manager`): assets, the CMDB,
   software licences and discovery results.
3. **New built-in roles:**
   - Change manager (`change_manager`; lane A declares its change permissions, WP-045), which
     also runs the change advisory boards and may make an emergency override
     (`changes.manage_boards`, `changes.emergency_override`, #327);
   - Release manager (`release_manager`; lane C);
   - Asset manager (lane B);
   - Purchase and contract manager (`purchase_manager`; WP-054);
   - Department viewer (`department_viewer`, D-152).
   - CAB membership stays per board.
4. **Every built-in role** has a name, a description and a default scope (labels en/ar/ur), shown
   on the roles screen. Any role can be copied into a custom role. **A built-in role can't be
   deleted:** the server answers 409 `system_role_not_deletable`.

**Existing tenants (migration 0121).** There are no customers yet, so the change applies directly.
The migration runs per tenant with `app.tenant_id` set, logs per tenant how many people went each
way, and is reversible.
- A holder of the old service desk manager moves to Team lead when the tenant's technicians see
  their groups (`access_settings.technician_scope = 'group'`, also the default when unanswered),
  or when the holder leads a live group. Everyone else stays Service desk manager.
- Decided by the coordinator under Haider's "map by current scope". The built-in role only ever
  held all-records grants, so no per-holder scope was stored to read.
- The technician's narrower asset rights are code, with no data step, and go in the release notes.

**Standing rule:** after the first customer install, any change that reduces existing rights needs
a release-note announcement and a mapping that removes nothing silently.

## Rationale

One role mixed running a team with running the desk. Split, each role holds what its job needs,
and the per-role matrix test pins each role's exact rights, so a new permission is placed on
purpose.
