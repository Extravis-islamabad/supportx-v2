# D-152 (2026-11-10)

## Decision

**A team lead manages only the groups they lead, never tenant-wide settings.** Haider, through
the coordinator, 2026-11-10.

- **What a team lead manages in its own groups:**
  - members' availability;
  - the group's assignment rules and round-robin;
  - its reply templates, saved views and dashboards.
- **How it is checked:** a new permission `groups.manage_own`, held by the built-in Team lead role.
  Every endpoint in scope allows `groups.manage` (every group: admins), or `groups.manage_own` for
  a group the caller leads (`support_groups.lead_id`). The check runs on the server, in
  organization's `require_group_manager` / `may_manage_group`. Disabled or deleted groups are led
  by nobody.
- **Refusals:**
  - another group's settings: 403 `group_not_led` (params: `group_id`);
  - another group's records: 404, as the record scopes already make them.
- **What else a team lead sees:** changes, assets and the CMDB, read-only, by default.
- **Department heads** see their department's requests in the portal (`work_items.view_department`),
  read-only and without technician fields.
  - This is on by default for a department's head. It is computed from `departments.head_id` when
    permissions are compiled, so a new head has it at once and a replaced one loses it.
  - Admins grant it to anyone else with the built-in Department viewer role. Granting and revoking
    are audited (`user.role_assigned`, `user.role_removed`).
- `work_items.view_reports` stays custom-role only.
- **Wiring:**
  - lane B: the role model, the check, members' availability;
  - lane A: assignment rules, round-robin, reply templates, saved views;
  - lane C: group dashboards.
  - Lane B gives the blocking review on each.

## Rationale

Group leads run their own team day to day, but tenant-wide settings belong to administrators. One
server-side check keeps every module's rule the same. Computing the department head's right from
the stored head means no role to remember to move when the head changes.
