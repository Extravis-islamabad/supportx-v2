# D-125 (2026-11-02)

## Decision

**WP-034 plan approved: knowledge search, suggestions and portal knowledge** (plan
~/Desktop/wp-034-plan.md; approved by the coordinator for the product owner, 2026-11-02; one PR
in two sessions, the portal pages after WP-028). (1) **One visibility rule everywhere:** search,
suggestions, "popular", the portal's topics and home lists all go through the article list's own
reader rule, so a hidden article never appears in any of them (coordinator's condition, with a
test across readers). (2) **Search (REQ-KB-053):** topic names count at query time (a generated
column cannot read another table); typos through pg_trgm on the title; snippets as text and marks,
never HTML; filters audience, updated from/to and featured. (3) **One suggestion service
(REQ-KB-062)** for the console form, the portal form and later the chat assistant: published only,
any word, ranked by how many match; category, service and CI matching is REQ-KB-011 (V1);
in-progress suggestions REQ-KB-066 (V1, off). Setting `suggest_on_technician_form`, on by default;
off hides the console panel and the portal still suggests. (4) **Suggestions on the new incident
form (REQ-INC-021):** a fixed-width column beside the form so it never moves (REQ-WI-012), 400 ms
pause; links chosen before the record exists are made after the create with source
`suggestion`. (5) **Did this help? (REQ-KB-069):** table `kb_votes`, one vote per person and
published version, changed in place; reasons are label codes (steps did not work, out of date,
hard to follow, not what I needed, other) and an optional plain-text comment of at most 1,000
characters; scores and feedback (never who) for those who edit or approve the article, never
requesters; a new version starts at zero; `KbVoteSaved` refreshes the counts live; no audit (not a
privileged action). (6) **Q1 Popular** = helpful votes plus record links in the last 90 days (no
page-view counting at MVP). **Q2** the portal's result-group order belongs to the portal's
settings (lane A); knowledge adds only `portal_search` on/off; until then the order is fixed:
requests, knowledge, catalog, announcements. **Q3 Featured** is a flag on the article set by its
editors. **Q4** comments (REQ-KB-071), known-issue pages (REQ-KB-041), in-progress suggestions
and category/service/CI matching are V1. (7) **Portal API built now** (topics with readable
articles, home lists); while requester knowledge is off every requester call answers 404
(REQ-KB-081 AC2). (8) The knowledge screens move onto the console layout of D-124 in this WP
(coordinator, 2026-11-03). Numbered D-123 on the branch first; #160 took D-123 on main.

## Rationale

Every place that offers an article must answer the same question the article page answers, or a
reader learns that hidden articles exist. Building the portal-facing reads and the vote model
before the portal keeps session 2 to screens only.
