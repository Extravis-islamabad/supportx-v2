# D-053 (2026-10-24)

## Decision

An assigner may hand an incident to a group outside their own scope, after a clear warning that they will lose sight of it (409 `would_lose_access`, confirmed by `confirm_lose_access`); the hand-over is recorded in history.

## Rationale

Hand-over between teams is normal work, not a scope escape.
