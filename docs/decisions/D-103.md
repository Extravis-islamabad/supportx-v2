Priority after #70: backend CI under 15 minutes (parallel tests or shards, slow tests nightly only), before session 3.** (1) **A second Keycloak service account, `supportx-realm`, holding only `manage-realm`**, because the password policy is a realm setting and `supportx-identity` must not be able to change the realm (verified on 26.8.1: the identity account gets 403). Its secret (`keycloak-realm-secret`) goes to the **consumers worker only**; the API saves the policy and the worker applies it through the outbox (`password_policy.changed`), recording applied or failed with a reason and an "Apply again" button. Never retried silently. (2) **The password policy lives in `identity_settings`** with the other sign-in settings; on single-tenant installations it is the installation's policy (D-089 D1 c); multi-tenant installations refuse changes (`password_policy_platform`). Ranges: length 8 to 64, history 0 to 24, expiry 0 to 365 days; `maxLength(128)`, `notUsername` and `notEmail` are always on. (3) **The breached list** is SecLists' top 100,000 (MIT), filtered to printable ASCII of 8 or more characters, lowercased (46,941 entries, about 450 KB), shipped in the chart as a ConfigMap and in Compose as a mount; the golden chart render shows its checksum. A realm still on the policy shipped before this session is moved to the new default by the deploy step; any other policy is kept. (4) **`PUT /identity/settings` keeps fields left out** (each card saves only its own fields), so one card never resets another's. (5) **Invitations are sent from the person's record** (WP-021's drawer, agreed with lane A) and listed, resent and withdrawn on Admin > Sign-in and security (newest 200); live on the list channel `invitations` (users.manage). (6) **Keycloak's login messages:** the SupportX login theme adds the password-rule messages in Urdu (Keycloak ships Arabic, not Urdu), reads Urdu right to left, and says why a breached password is refused. (7) **CI:** the backend job's timeout is 45 minutes (the test step reached 30; agreed with lane A).
