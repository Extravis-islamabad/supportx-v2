# D-137 (2026-11-04)

## Decision

**Fonts bundled with the app** (UI pass 2; the product owner, through the coordinator,
2026-11-04).

- Fonts ship with the app and are never loaded from Google's (or any) servers: the product runs
  air-gapped. All are SIL OFL 1.1.
- **Inter** for Latin; **Noto Sans Arabic** for Arabic.
- **Urdu:** Noto Sans Arabic (Naskh) by default in the UI, with **Noto Nastaliq Urdu** as a
  per-person option under My profile › Display and region, next to Digits. It is stored like
  digits: nullable, with the default applied when read (`users.urdu_script`, in migration 0072).
- Subset to keep the bundle small: Latin, Latin Extended and Vietnamese for Inter (customers'
  names in Turkish, Polish, Vietnamese and more); the Arabic script range with its digits and
  punctuation for both Noto faces. woff2 only, `font-display: swap`.
- Set in one place: `packages/ui/src/fonts/fonts.ts` holds the @font-face rules and the family
  tokens, switched by language and Urdu style.
- Nastaliq gets taller lines so nothing clips (tags, table rows, inputs, the menu, the header at
  390 px), checked with axe and in the screenshots.
- The OFL licence texts ship with the fonts (`/licenses/fonts.md`).
- Delivery: the @fontsource packages (`@fontsource/inter`, `@fontsource/noto-sans-arabic`,
  `@fontsource/noto-nastaliq-urdu`, 5.3.0; fonts OFL-1.1, packaging MIT).

## Rationale

The target design uses Inter, but loading it from Google breaks air-gapped installs and
carries no Arabic or Urdu. Bundled, subset faces give one look in every language with a small
download, and Nastaliq stays available for those who read Urdu best in it.
