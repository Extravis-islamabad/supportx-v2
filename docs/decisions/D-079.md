# D-079 (2026-10-26)

## Decision

**WP-020b defaults** (stated in the WP-020b plan; the product owner gave the scope and did not object): (1) **notes are private by default**; replies are always public. (2) **Hand-over keeps following by default** (tenant setting `keep_following_on_handover`, D-068): the follower gets **view and private notes only** for that record, through the shared policy layer (`ResourceType.shared_scope`), never edit, assign or status. (3) **First response** is stamped by a public reply or public note by default; admins choose (`first_response_on`, screen in WP-020c). (4) **Mentions** add the person as a follower only if they can already see the record; otherwise the author is warned and nobody is notified. Following never widens access except after a hand-over. (5) **Notifications** for replies, notes, mentions and followers come from the outbox events in WP-023; **replies are saved and marked "not sent: email channel not configured"** until WP-024. (6) **Reply templates**: a per-tenant table, managed by admins from the composer until the WP-020c settings screens. (7) **Personal counters**: overdue, due today, waiting on me, updated by others (last 24 hours), unassigned in my groups; each is also a list view. "Pending my approval" comes with approvals (WP-030). (8) Record tabs in the fixed order **Conversation, Details, History**, opening on Details. (9) The **richer editor** stores alignment, indent, colours and highlights as codes (`data-*`, fixed palettes, logical start/end), never inline styles; inline images wait for WP-022.

## Rationale

Product owner's WP-020b scope; defaults chosen to be least surprising and least privileged.
