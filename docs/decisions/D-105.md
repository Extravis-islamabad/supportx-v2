# D-105 (2026-10-28)

## Decision

**WP-029-FIX app-wide rules** (product owner's WP-029-FIX brief; pending review). (1) **Refusals vs failures:** a 4xx answer with a reason (a rule, a validation, a permission, a limit) is an expected refusal: shown once, in the user's language, at the form, dialog or drawer that saved (`FormProblem`), or as a neutral notice for one-click actions; never a red error toast with "Copy reference". Red toasts with the reference are kept for real failures (5xx, outages, unknown). Each error is shown once however many places report it. (2) **Error labels take their params** for every code; CI checks that every `errors.*` placeholder is filled (UI) and that every raise site sends the params its labels name (backend AST check). (3) **Plurals:** every counted label (English plural families, or `{{count}}` inside a sentence) has all six Arabic forms and one/other in Urdu and English (CI check); a number shown as a value ("People: {{count}}") needs none. (4) **Conflicts:** every 409 `version_conflict` names who saved last and when (from the row), plus the fields where the record keeps field history (`stale_version`); every editor opens the one shared conflict dialog, which compares the opened and latest versions when the server cannot name the fields. (5) **Availability:** a technician, the lead of a group they are in, or anyone with `users.manage` sets them Available or Away; each change is kept in the field history and shown as "Set Away by <name> at <time>" on the person page, the group editor and the lead's "Groups you lead" card. (6) A move limited to certain people names them (roles, groups, people on the record).
