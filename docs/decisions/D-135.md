# D-135 (2026-11-04)

## Decision

**Numbers on screen** (REQ-ADM-059, WP-042; the product owner, through the coordinator,
2026-11-04).

- **Western digits (0-9) by default in every language**, Arabic and Urdu included.
- **A per-person option for Arabic-Indic digits** (٠-٩), in My profile › Display and region.
- Thousands and decimal separators and date formats follow the person's language.

## Rationale

Most users in the target markets read Western digits in business software, and mixed digits in
IDs and SLA timers confuse. Those who prefer Arabic-Indic digits can switch them on for
themselves.

PROGRESS.md (WP-042 session 3), migration 0072 (the nullable `digits` preference on users).
