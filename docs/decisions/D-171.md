# D-171 (2026-11-13)

## Decision

**GDPR person erasure and export: what goes, what stays, who decides** (Haider, the product
owner, through the coordinator; lane B's plan, ADR-052).

- **D1a. Text they wrote on organisation records stays.** Replies, notes and work logs a person
  wrote are the organisation's record; they stay, shown with the erased name.
- **D2a. Records they requested lose the requester's personal text.** Subject, description,
  custom text, `personal_data` custom fields and answers are replaced by the one reserved marker
  `erased_on_request` (shown as "Removed at the person's request"); technicians' replies stay.
- **D3a. Costs.** The person's cost rates are deleted; worklog amounts already worked out stay.
- **D4. Shared things they own pass to a successor** (reports, dashboards, workflows, boards,
  shared templates, groups they lead, departments they head, asset ownership), never deleted or
  orphaned. The successor is named at the request; if they have left by the time the job runs,
  the approving admin takes over, named in the erasure's record.
- **D5. Keycloak.** The person leaves this tenant's organisation; their account is deleted only
  when no other tenant has them.
- **D6b. The erased name** is "Erased person" with a stable 4-character code derived from the
  record ID (not reversible to anything personal), so people can still tell erased rows apart.
- **D7. No self-service erasure.** A person asks through an ordinary request; admins act on it.
- **D8. Timing.** The erasure job itself completes in hours. A request waits at most 7 days for a
  second admin's approval (then it expires), and the screens show the one-month GDPR deadline
  counted from the request. The export (REQ-ADM-100) is a ZIP (`people.export`, a recent
  confirmation, audited; the export-job pattern), offered as "export first" before an erasure.
- **H1. Legal hold** on a person, set and lifted by tenant admins (`audit.manage_holds`), each
  audited with the reason; a held person is never erased. Until record-level holds (WP-064 S3)
  this person-level check is the whole hold, and it fails closed.
- **Two people.** One admin requests (reason code and text, successor, files to keep with a
  reason each, the person's name typed); a different admin approves (the name typed again);
  both confirm it is them. A request expires after 7 days. Erasing yourself, the last tenant
  admin, a break-glass account or a held person is refused. `people.erase`, `people.export` and
  `audit.manage_holds` are held by the built-in tenant admin role only.

Haider's additions:

- **(a) A mentions report before approval.** The approver sees where the person is named in
  other people's text. Until it exists (S1b), approval is refused server-side.
- **(b) Uploaded attachments are deleted by default**, unless the admin keeps a file with a
  reason (audited per file).
- **(c) A starter catalog item** "Personal data request (export or erasure)", built by lane A,
  creates an ordinary request for admins.

## Rationale

An erasure touches every module and outside systems (Keycloak, mail), and cannot be undone, so
it needs two people, a record of every step, and a job that resumes rather than leaving a person
half-erased. Keeping the organisation's own record (D1a, D3a's amounts, IDs that still resolve)
while removing what identifies the person is the balance GDPR art. 17 allows; a legal hold
overrides an erasure. Approval waits for the mentions report so the approver knows what the
erasure will not reach.
