# D-049 (2026-10-24)

## Decision

**AI model access, pending a pricing decision:** SaaS includes a **fair-use allowance** of cloud model usage; **Enterprise** may **bring its own key**; **on-prem and air-gapped** use a **local model**. Final allowances and prices are open (product-plan.md §11.2, pricing levels). Detailed by D-064.

## Rationale

AI is included in every tier (product-plan.md §9.1) without unbounded model cost.
