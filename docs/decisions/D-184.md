# D-184 (2026-11-24)

## Decision

**A service request carries an estimated cost, and who sees it is fixed.** Haider, the product
owner, answered WP-047a's plan question 3 (through the coordinator):

- A catalog item may have a price. A request's **estimated cost** is the price (with any chosen
  options' prices) times the quantity, **snapshotted on the request** when it is raised (and
  again when its answers or quantity change), never moved by later edits of the item.
- The estimate is shown to the request's **approvers** and to technicians holding
  **`service_requests.view_costs`**.
- **Requesters** see it (and item prices) only when the tenant turns that on: a catalog setting,
  **off by default**.
- It is in the tenant's **base currency** (D-164's base-currency rule; Haider wrote "D-163", but
  D-163 is the NodeLocal DNS cache, so the reference is to D-164 item 2).
- The **actual cost** (worklogs and task costs) stays `view_costs` only.

It replaces REQ-SR-043 AC3's "show cost to approvers" toggle: approvers always see the estimate.

## Rationale

Approvers decide on what something costs, so they need the figure; requesters see it only where
the organisation chooses (some tenants do not show internal prices). A snapshot keeps an approved
request's figure stable when the catalog changes later. One base currency keeps every total
comparable, as for asset and worklog costs (D-164).
