# D-185 (2026-11-24)

## Decision

**Deleting an asset (moving it to the trash) is its own right, `assets.delete`, never held by
vendor support and audited.** Haider, the product owner, decided it through the coordinator, on
the D-173 finding that vendor support could delete assets through `assets.manage`.

- `DELETE /assets/{asset_id}` needs `assets.delete` alone; `assets.manage` no longer stands in.
- It is `never` for vendor support (a tenant decision), at every access level.
- The built-in asset manager holds it by default; tenant admins hold every right.
- Migration 0202 gives `assets.delete` to every role that held `assets.manage`, at the same scope
  and condition, so nobody loses the delete they had. Its log counts, per tenant, how many of
  those roles are held by a service identity (a service account or a workflow's run-as identity),
  so an admin can review automation that can now delete.
- Deleting is audited as before (`asset.deleted`), refusals included.

## Rationale

`assets.manage` covers adding and changing assets, which vendor support may be granted at "view
and change". Deleting is destructive and the tenant's own decision, so it can no longer ride on
that right: the same split #610 made for configuration items (`cmdb.delete`).
