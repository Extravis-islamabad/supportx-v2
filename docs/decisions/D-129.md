# D-129 (2026-11-04)

## Decision

**WP-028: requesters' wider reach, and who opens the console** (product owner, through the
coordinator, 2026-11-04).

1. **Wider reach (WP-028 plan D2, option a).** Beyond their own records (requester or the person
   it is for), a person sees more in the portal through two permissions of the **work-item
   engine**, not of one type, so service requests use them too: `work_items.view_department`
   (their own department's records) and `work_items.view_reports` (their direct reports',
   under a filter of its own). No role holds them by default. Site and all-records reach stay on
   `requests.view`'s scope. One scope check decides it (`work_items/service/portal.py:
   viewer()`); RLS and out-of-scope tests cover it.
2. **Who opens the console (replaces REQ-PRT-001 AC1 as built on #178 and agreed with lane B on
   2026-11-04).** Anyone holding **any** role other than `requester` (a technician or admin role,
   system or custom) lands on the console, which links to the requester portal ("Requester
   portal" in the user menu). **Requester-only** people (every role they hold is `requester`, or
   they hold none) always land on the portal and can never open the console. The server enforces
   it: the BFF never creates a console session for them (the browser goes to the portal's home),
   and `/auth/me` ends an existing console session of theirs (`console_requester_only`, 401); the
   console's guard is only the backstop. Roles are SupportX's (D-029), never Keycloak's.

   *Reading recorded for the product owner:* "any technician or admin role" is read as any role
   but `requester`, custom roles included, so a custom role is enough to open the console; a person
   with no role at all is treated as requester-only.

## Rationale

(1) One pair of permissions for the engine avoids a second pair when service requests arrive
(WP-030) and keeps "My requests" one list. (2) Deciding by role, not by the stored type, lets a
person who only approves or reads reports use the console without becoming a technician (no seat),
while a person with no staff role can never reach staff screens, whatever the routing does.
