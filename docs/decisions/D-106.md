# D-106 (2026-10-28)

## Decision

**WP-023 notification defaults** (plan ~/Desktop/wp-023-plan.md; file 03 Appendix A is *proposed*, so these are pending review). (1) **One engine through a port:** modules register a `RecordSource` in `app/shared/notifications/ports.py` (events, recipients relative to the record, variables, conditions, access); the consumer `notifications__dispatch` subscribes to every event and acknowledges unread the types no source reads (new `ConsumerSpec.accepts`, no claim row). Only listed senders call the mail sender (architecture test). (2) **Defaults:** file 01 Appendix A for incidents (D-041) and file 03 Appendix A: channels in-app and e-mail; required (cannot be switched off): assigned to me, mention (approvals join with WP-030); urgent: P1. Followers are default recipients of public replies and notes (REQ-INC-130 lists them). (3) **A public reply is itself the message to the requester** (WP-024 sends it), so the reply notification is in-app for the requester and e-mail for followers; a public note e-mails the requester. (4) **Access at send time:** staff who may see the record get full content; the record's requester and the person it is for get the public side only, even without a console permission (the portal rule); private-note events go only to people with full access, so a requester never even learns one exists; everyone else gets nothing, logged `no_access`. (5) **Never silent:** every message has a delivery row (sent, queued, waiting, failed, suppressed + reason); with no outgoing mail, e-mails wait rendered and admins see a banner with the count; they are sent when mail works (minute schedule), re-checking access and address. E-mail retries are the mail foundation's (5 attempts, backoff to 1 hour) rather than file 03's 6 over 24 hours (gap). (6) **Do-not-disturb** keeps in-app entries (no pop-up) and holds back e-mail that is neither required nor urgent. (7) **Session 2:** template editor with versions, preview and test send; throttling, coalescing, bulk summaries and digests; signed action links (incident confirm/reject with WP-020f); group subscriptions; retention (90/180 days); the incident log in lane A's History tab.
