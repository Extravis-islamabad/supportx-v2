# D-141 (2026-11-05)

## Decision

**Assets: what counts toward the limit, how cost is stored, and the label libraries** (WP-049; the
product owner, through the coordinator, 2026-11-05; answers to the WP-049 plan's open points).

- **Only IT assets count toward the licence's asset limit.** Non-IT assets are unlimited. Every
  product type carries a clear IT / non-IT flag (`category`: `it` or `non_it`) and a kind
  (`asset`, `component` or `consumable`), both set on the top-level type and inherited by the
  types below. The managed-asset counter counts assets whose type is IT and of kind `asset`.
- **Purchase cost is stored as price, tax and total.** Price and tax are kept apart and the total
  is worked out from them, never typed. An asset's cost includes tax by default; an admin setting
  (`cost_includes_tax`, default on) excludes it, and depreciation follows that setting.
- **`segno` and `python-barcode` are approved** for asset labels (QR codes and barcodes). The label
  PDF still sits behind a small interface, so either library can be swapped.

This replaces the plan's interim defaults (every non-disposed asset counts; purchase cost excludes
tax).

## Rationale

Non-IT assets (furniture, vehicles) are tracked for completeness, not managed like devices, so
charging for them would discourage recording them. Keeping price and tax apart lets a tax change
or a tax-exempt purchase be handled without rewriting prices, and lets each tenant choose which
figure its books depreciate.
