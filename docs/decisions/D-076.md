# D-076 (2026-10-24)

## Decision

**WP-018 audit log decisions** (plan approved): (1) **asynchronous sealing**; unsealed entries show "pending seal", and any entry unsealed longer than **5 minutes** raises an alert. (2) Anchors signed with an **Ed25519 installation key** in a Kubernetes secret, with **key rotation** (checkpoints signed by old keys stay verifiable); an **HSM/KMS signer** is recorded as a later option. (3) **Personal data outside the hash.** (4) **Shared object storage now**: the storage component (aioboto3, D-054) and a minimal single-node **SeaweedFS** in the Helm chart (dev and single-node profiles; Compose keeps its own); WP-022 reuses it; exports and archive **fail visibly**, never silently, when storage is unavailable. (5) Defaults: retention **3 years (min 1, max 10)**, **daily and weekly** verification, **10-minute** read window, alert after **10 refusals in 10 minutes**; all admin-editable, and every change to them is itself audited. (6) Built-in **Auditor** role with separation of duties: auditors read, search, export and verify and change nothing; admins cannot edit or delete entries; changing retention or settings is audited. (7) Keycloak-side events come with **WP-039**. (8) Until WP-023: **console banner plus monitoring alert**. (9) **3 sessions**. (10) Mixed retention in one monthly partition: after archiving, a tenant's rows past its retention are **hidden by an RLS cut-off**; the partition is **dropped only when every tenant in it has expired**. (11) **10M-row tests in the nightly suite.** (12) **ADR-027.** Implementation note: the chain is written by a migrator-owned SECURITY DEFINER function rather than a separate `supportx_audit` login role (stronger and one less credential; ADR-027).

## Rationale

Product owner review of the WP-018 plan.
