# D-164 (2026-11-12)

## Decision

**Who sees money on a record, and in which currency.** Haider, through the coordinator,
2026-11-12, answering lane A's WP-046a S3 questions. Built by lane A.

1. **`<type>.view_costs`** is the one right for money on a record: worklog cost rates,
   technician and other costs, cost totals, and a task's extra cost (REQ-INC-068 AC2), on
   incidents, service requests, problems, changes and releases. Without it the API, the screens
   and exports show none of it. By default:
   - tenant administrators (who hold every right) and the **service desk manager**, on every
     type;
   - the **change manager**, on changes;
   - the **release manager**, on releases.

   Team leads do not get it by default; an administrator can grant it to any role.
2. **Base currency only.** Every cost is stored in the tenant's base currency. A line paid in
   another currency keeps its original amount, currency and the rate the person entered, and the
   amount is worked out from them to the cent. There are no automatic exchange rates.
3. **Cost rates (people's hourly cost; lane B, #427 and its follow-up).**
   - `users.view_cost_rates` (see a person's cost rate) and `users.manage_cost_rates` (set or end
     one) are held by the tenant admin role only, by default. Both can be added to a custom role,
     for example "Finance". No other built-in role holds them.
   - Every view and every change of a person's rate is audited. Each read of a person's rate
     through the API or the person card writes one audit entry: who, whose rate, when, and from
     where. A save writes one entry with the old and the new rate. Listing several people's
     rates writes one entry per person shown.
   - The system's own snapshot of a rate is not a view and is not audited: that's the cost_rate
     port, which stamps a worklog's cost when it's saved. The audit entry of the worklog save
     itself covers it.
   - Rates are in the tenant's base currency only. A rate saved in another currency is never used
     for costing (the port returns none), and the screen says so.

## Rationale

Cost rates are personal and pay-related, so money is shown only to those who manage the work's
cost, and the right follows the managers of each type. One currency keeps totals and reports
exact without a rate feed, which on-prem and air-gapped installations may not have; the
original amount and rate keep what was actually paid.
