## Decision

**WP-033 session 4 plan approved** (plan ~/Desktop/wp-033-s4-plan.md; approved by the coordinator for the product owner, with lane A's answers to Q1-Q2 and lane B's to Q3, 2026-11-02). (1) Knowledge links to incidents in their own table `kb_record_links` (separate from `work_item_links`); the incident shows its readable articles plus "N restricted" in a **Knowledge card in the side panel**. (2) **Incident visibility through a shared port** `app/shared/records/ports.py` that work_items registers at import (lane C writes it, lane A reviews): `visible` uses exactly the rule of `GET /incidents/{id}` (scope, trash, requester and on-behalf-of), at most 500 IDs; `requester_of` returns the requester and on-behalf-of; `resolution_of` returns subject, sanitised resolution and language, or nothing. Knowledge never reads incident tables. (3) **Apply to a reply:** a picker in the composer through lane A's `knowledgeSlot` prop; insert content or a link; the "not visible to the requester" warning comes only from the server's answer. Images are copied into the actor's drafts by `copy_to_drafts` in the files service (lane B's conditions: only files linked to that article, openable sources only and the refused ones named, sha256 checked while re-encrypting under the new ID, thumbnails too, `files.copied_from` recorded, the tenant's limits plus 25 MB per insert, one quota lock, all or nothing, the upload event with source `knowledge`); copies never become readable by anyone who could not read the article. (4) **Save as knowledge** from a resolution: the author picks the topic, the last one used pre-selected; no category → topic mapping (V1 at the earliest); a personal-data check warns in the draft and never blocks. (5) Migration 0048: `kb_record_links` (RLS) and `files.copied_from`.
