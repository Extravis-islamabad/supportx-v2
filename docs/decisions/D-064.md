# D-064 (2026-10-24)

## Decision

**AI access** (details D-049): bring your own key is **Enterprise only**; other on-prem tenants use the **local model**; when a SaaS tenant's fair-use allowance runs out, AI **falls back to the local model with a visible notice**. Allowance size still pending the pricing decision.

## Rationale

Predictable cost without AI silently stopping.
