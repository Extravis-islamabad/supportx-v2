# D-037 (2026-10-24)

## Decision

Approved deployment components (all Apache-2.0, MIT or BSD): **CloudNativePG** operator (PostgreSQL on Kubernetes), **cert-manager**, the official **NATS** chart and prometheus-nats-exporter, the official **Valkey** chart and redis_exporter, **Gateway API** CRDs, **Traefik** (bundled with k3s), **kubeconform** (CI), **External Secrets Operator** (SaaS), **Fluent Bit**, **OpenTelemetry Collector**, **Zot** (air-gapped registry), **rclone** (object-store backups), **Kyverno** or the Sigstore policy-controller (later). On-prem monitoring: **VictoriaMetrics, VictoriaLogs, Jaeger, Perses** (D-038).

## Rationale

Components of the WP-014 deployment plan; permissive licences allow on-prem redistribution (§5 licence box).
