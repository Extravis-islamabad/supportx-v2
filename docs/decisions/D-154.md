# D-154 (2026-11-10)

## Decision

**Assignment stays inside the record's group, and a request's tasks move it and name who did
them** (the product owner, through the coordinator, 2026-11-10; QA10-P1-07 and P4-40).

- **a. The assignee is always in the record's group, for everyone and all work-item types**
  (incidents, problems, changes, service requests and releases).
  - Someone of another group is assigned only by moving the record to that person's group in
    the same request. Otherwise the server refuses with `technician_not_in_group` on the
    technician field.
  - No role skips this any more. The engine's `<type>.assign_outside_group` permission is
    removed, and migration 0125 drops every role grant of it (built-in and custom), counting
    them per tenant. Its downgrade is schema-only: the grants are not restored, as there are no
    real customers yet.
  - In the console, picking someone outside the group offers one action, "Move to <group> and
    assign to <person>". If the person is in several groups, the user picks the group.
  - The hand-over warning applies (D-053), and the original technician keeps following by
    default (D-068).
- **c. Ticking a request's first fulfilment task moves it to In progress.**
  - A New or Assigned service request moves when the task is ticked by the request's own
    technician or someone of its group.
  - The move is recorded in its history as automatic.
  - A tenant setting turns it off: on by default, audited when changed, labelled in English,
    Arabic and Urdu.
- **d. Tasks say who did them.** Nothing is assigned automatically. Each done task shows
  "Done by <name>", recorded in its history.
- **E1 follows d** (the coordinator, from Haider's screen review, 2026-11-10): a request is
  "Fulfilled" only with a technician on it.
  - A manual Fulfil without one is refused with a clear field message.
  - When the last required task is done and nobody is on the request, it is not fulfilled by
    itself. It shows "Ready to fulfil: assign someone", and its group gets one in-app notice.
  - Nobody is assigned automatically. This is built in lane A's screen-review bundle.

## Rationale

A technician handing an incident to someone outside its group left the record in a group whose
members could not see who works it, and the holder of `assign_outside_group` could do that
silently. Moving the record with the person keeps the queue, the SLA's group and the reports
right. A request whose work has started should say so without a second click; naming who
finished each task replaces guessing from the history.

## Affected docs

None of the guides named the removed permission. The role editor no longer lists "Assign outside
the group" for any record type (its labels are gone with it).
