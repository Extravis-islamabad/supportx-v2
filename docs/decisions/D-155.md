# D-155 (2026-11-10)

## Decision

**UI fix round, Haider 2026-11-10.** The product owner reviewed screenshots of INC-66, the
incidents list, REL-3 (Plan, Downtime), SR-11 and the "New laptop" form and asked for every item
to be fixed, through the coordinator. The template-level fixes (lane C, D-147's templates) reach
every module. Three of them change what a requirement says:

1. **The selection bar appears only while rows are selected, in place of the toolbar.** With no
   selection the list shows one toolbar row (view, search, quick filters, Filters; Columns at the
   end), not the bulk actions disabled with a reason. This supersedes the "disabled until
   something is selected, with the reason" part of REQ-INC-119 AC2 (parity
   01-work-items-incidents.md, REQ-INC-119). Selecting across pages ("all N") is unchanged.
2. **One Reply on a record.** The header keeps the primary Reply, with reply to all and forward in
   its dropdown; the conversation toolbar's own Reply goes, and Add note stays. This overrides
   the earlier "keep both" reading of REQ-INC-058 (parity 01-work-items-incidents.md).
3. **One date rule.** It refines REQ-WI-014 (the one shared formatter), REQ-ADM-049 (tenant
   date formats) and REQ-DSH-044 (zones in reports):
   - by default (no choice made), dates show unambiguously in the person's language, e.g.
     "10 Nov 2026, 11:35" in English, never "10/11/2026";
   - a numeric format the person picked in their profile shows on their own screens only;
   - the tenant default and everything shared (e-mails, PDFs, the audit log) always use the
     unambiguous form;
   - CSV exports write ISO 8601 ("2026-11-10 11:35"), with the time zone in the column header
     ("Created (Asia/Karachi)");
   - Excel exports write real date cells (typed datetimes) with a display format, never text, so
     spreadsheets sort and filter them.

The rest of the round (one record header for every type, the filled-fields panel, chip spacing,
the spacing scale, disabled buttons that say why, the release record's header, Plan and Downtime
tabs, and the proof shots) carries out D-147's templates and changes no requirement.

## Rationale

The screens showed two control rows above a list, a reply offered twice, and numeric dates that
read differently in different countries (10/11 is 10 November or 11 October). One toolbar, one
reply and one unambiguous date form make every module read the same, and typed dates keep
exports usable in spreadsheets.
