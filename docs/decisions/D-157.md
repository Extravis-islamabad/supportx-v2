# D-157 (2026-11-11)

## Decision

**Technicians on their tickets' assets, money behind its right, straight-line depreciation by
default, and one notice when mail was never set up.** Haider, through the coordinator,
2026-11-11. Built by lane B.

1. **A technician changes a few fields of the assets of its own tickets** (completes D-153
   item 2). A technician holds `assets.view`, not `assets.manage`, and no new permission is
   added. It may change an asset only while both hold:
   - the asset is linked to a ticket assigned to it that is still being worked: open, paused,
     or resolved and not closed (the coordinator, 2026-11-10; a closed, canceled or trashed
     ticket grants nothing);
   - the asset is held by that ticket's requester or the person it was raised on behalf of, or
     by one of their departments (lane A's review added the on-behalf person).

   Even then only its **state** (a move along its life cycle), its **location**, its **notes**
   (open to anyone who sees the asset, REQ-AST-050) and its **fitted components** (fitting a part
   nobody holds whose product type is of kind component, or taking one out) change; a move never
   goes to a retired or disposed state that way (`asset_move_not_allowed_through_ticket`), and
   fitting anything but a free component is refused (`asset_not_a_component`; lane B's review).
   Never its holder, assignment, financials (price, tax,
   depreciation, cost centre, GL code, costs) or anything else: the server refuses with
   `asset_field_not_allowed` (403, `params.field`). Each such change keeps history and audit as
   usual and shows as made by the technician. Someone with `assets.manage` is unaffected.
2. **Money behind `assets.view_financials`.** An asset's purchase price, tax and total and its
   depreciation settings (method, useful life, salvage value) are read and written only with
   `assets.view_financials` (asset managers and administrators by default):
   - asset responses (get, list, held-by, groups, links) return them as null for others; the
     fields stay in the API (no property removed);
   - the asset list never filters or sorts by them (for anyone); report money fields stay for
     holders only;
   - the asset's history keeps those lines for others but masks their values (`hidden: true`),
     so they still see that the price changed, when and by whom;
   - writing the price or tax needs the right, as depreciation already did.
   **2b. Catalogue prices too** (the coordinator for Haider, 2026-11-11). A product's list
   price, tax and total and its default depreciation (method, useful life, salvage value) are
   null in every product response for people without `assets.view_financials`, and writing them
   needs the right as well as the catalogue's own. Product pickers keep working for everyone who
   may see the catalogue, without prices.
3. **Straight line is the default depreciation.** When neither the asset nor its product names a
   method, the asset depreciates straight line (shown as "Default (straight line)"). Declining
   balance takes its factor from the asset's product type (`depreciation_factor`, 2 by default,
   more than 0 and at most 10, set in Admin > Assets > Types). Double declining balance stays a
   fixed 200 % (lane B's choice: assets that chose it keep their figures whatever their type's
   factor; declining balance with the default factor gives the same figures). Migration 0136.
4. **Mail not set up: one notice to the admins per run.** When the waiting schedule gives up
   e-mails that waited 7 days for outgoing mail to be set up (D-116, never sent late), it also
   sends **one** in-app notice per run to each person who may set mail up
   (`integrations.manage_email`, administrators by default): how many, the oldest date, and a
   link to Admin > Mail. Never one per e-mail, and none when a run gives none up; a retried
   run never sends it twice (claimed once per tenant and cut-off minute). In the app only:
   there is no mail to send it with.

## Rationale

D-153 left technicians view-only on assets until records could link them (REQ-AST-025, #357).
Now they can, a technician at the desk needs to record where a laptop is, its state and a swapped
part, without the rights that change who holds it or what it cost. Tying the right to the ticket
and to the requester's own holdings keeps it narrow and lets it end with the ticket. Money is
sensitive and was readable by anyone with `assets.view`; one right now governs all of it.
Straight line is what most organisations book; the declining factor differs by kind of asset, so
it belongs on the type. Without the summary an administrator could not learn that e-mails were
being dropped, and one notice per e-mail would flood them.
