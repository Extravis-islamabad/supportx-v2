# D-090 (2026-10-27)

## Decision

**WP-017 defaults** (stated in the WP-017 plan per D-074; the product owner gave the scope): (1) **SVG is refused** for brand images (it can carry scripts): PNG, JPEG, WebP or ICO, scanned by the files service, re-encoded with Pillow. (2) **A colour failing WCAG AA is refused**, with the failing checks named and the **nearest passing colour offered in one click** (never changed silently); hover, dark-mode and high-contrast shades are derived as for SupportX (D-078). (3) The **high-contrast and dark logos are generated** from a cut-out logo when not uploaded. (4) Branding and custom domains in **every edition** (the requirements say Essentials); no licence gate. (5) A custom domain is proved by an **HTTP token** this installation answers with an HMAC (no DNS library); only verified domains resolve. (6) On a tenant's own address the sign-in asks Keycloak for **that organization only** (`organization:<alias>`, the alias is the tenant slug), so the session there holds that tenant only; elsewhere sign-in is unchanged. (7) On-prem single node may **upload its own certificate** (checked, key encrypted with the tenant's data key). **Open:** who creates certificates and routes for custom domains in Kubernetes (plan question).

WP-017 plan.
