# D-077 (2026-10-26)

## Decision

**Field history retention:** a record's field history (`field_changes`) is kept **as long as the record exists**; when a record is permanently purged, its history is **purged with it** and the purge is **audited**. Monthly partitioning of `field_changes` comes later.
