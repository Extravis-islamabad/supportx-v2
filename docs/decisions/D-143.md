# D-143 (2026-11-05)

## Decision

**Portal settings and the portal's defaults** (WP-032; the coordinator for the product owner,
2026-11-05, approving the WP-032 plan).

- The portal settings with no other home live in **work_items**, in a tenant-level
  `work_item_portal_settings` row (migration 0096, RLS):
  - the one search box's sources and their order (REQ-PRT-003), by default catalog items, help
    articles, announcements, then the person's own requests;
  - whether requesters may edit their own profile, on by default;
  - whether requesters get reminders of what waits for them, on by default.
- Settings that already live elsewhere stay there. They are read and saved on the one portal
  settings page through their own endpoints, and each change is audited where it lives:
  - the service request and incident type settings (on-behalf scope, approvals hidden from
    requesters, sign-in for approval links, due times shown to requesters);
  - the catalog's "hide the general incident form";
  - knowledge visibility.
- "Most requested" on the portal home counts service requests per item over the last **90 days**.
- Bulk approval from the approvals inbox takes at most **20** at a time, approve only. The
  portal inbox's "decided" list covers the last **30 days**.
- Public pages, guest requests and self-registration (REQ-PRT-025..027) and showing cost
  (REQ-CAT, WP-047) appear on the settings page as "later" until they ship.
- **Preview as a person** (REQ-PRT-032, WP-032 S3) was approved by the product owner later on
  2026-11-05, through the coordinator, on these conditions:
  - only admins with a dedicated permission (`users.preview`) may preview, after an MFA
    step-up;
  - it is strictly read-only, enforced on the server: every non-GET request is refused, GETs run
    in a read-only transaction, and nothing is written to Valkey (no presence, read markers or
    caches for the person);
  - it never uses the person's session, tokens or Keycloak;
  - a banner shows whom, the time left and an End button, with a time limit of 15 minutes by
    default and 30 at most (a tenant setting);
  - every preview is audited: start, end, how it ended, and the route templates and file ids
    seen;
  - the person gets no notifications;
  - **no previewing anyone with more privileges** (his addition): the server compares
    permission sets and allows equal or lower only, so admins, security admins and holders of
    the preview right are never previewed;
  - **"An admin previewed your view"** shows in the person's security activity, **on by
    default**, with a tenant switch to turn it off (his addition).

  Lane B builds the read-only session and the step-up in identity_access (migration 0084). Lane
  A builds the console and portal side as its own PR, an approved exception to one PR per WP
  (S1-S2 shipped as #220).

## Rationale

work_items already holds the requester-facing behaviour (`portal_show_due_time`). One small row
for what has no home keeps a single owner per setting, and the page still shows them all together
(REQ-PRT-031 AC1). The numbers are the plan's defaults: long enough to show real popularity, and
small enough for a confirmation that names every request.
