# D-156 (2026-11-11)

## Decision

**Change windows and change replies, Haider 2026-11-11** (the coordinator's morning questions;
the number was held for these since WP-045c).

1. **Windows keep the defaults ADR-042 named:** a freeze blocks, a maintenance window warns,
   and windows repeat weekly. Added:
   - **An emergency change in a blocking freeze can go through the emergency CAB** as well as
     through the change manager's freeze override. Saving its window never refuses it: the
     change is marked as in a freeze. Its move into implementation is refused until either
     (a) the emergency CAB approves it, with a reason, or (b) the change manager overrides the
     freeze, with a reason. Both are audited and shown in the change's history. Normal and
     standard changes are unchanged: blocked, or passed only by the override.
   - **A tenant setting makes "outside every maintenance window" block** instead of warn. The
     default stays warn. It sits on the change settings page (template E), in en/ar/ur, and
     saving it is audited.
   - **Monthly windows**, such as "first Sunday of the month" or "last Friday", besides weekly
     ones.
2. **Replies on changes are e-mailed** to the change's requester when it has one; notes never
   are. The change type's reply notice is on by default. Releases are lane C's to settle.
3. The technician asset-edit rule is D-157 (lane B).

## Rationale

Emergencies happen during freezes, and the emergency CAB is the body that already decides on
them. Some tenants run strictly inside maintenance windows. Real maintenance calendars are
monthly as often as weekly. And a change's requester should hear the answers written for them.
