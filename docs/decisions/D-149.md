# D-149 (2026-11-09)

## Decision

**Lane B's last three work packages move to lanes A and C** (the product owner, 2026-11-09, on the
coordinator's progress report).

- **WP-055 (integrations and ESM V1) and WP-056 (notifications V1) move to lane A.**
- **WP-057 (sign-in, MFA, sessions, network and API access, data protection, audit V1) moves to
  lane C.**
- **Lane B keeps:**
  - Assets (WP-049a/b) and CMDB with the MonetX read-only connector (WP-053);
  - discovery (WP-050), the SupportX Agent and remote access (WP-051);
  - software asset management (WP-052), purchase and contracts (WP-054);
  - WP-013 (licence core) and WP-014c (offline installer).
- **Condition:** lane B reviews every PR that touches identity, sign-in, sessions, MFA, secrets or
  security, in any lane.
  - The review is a PR comment ("Lane B reviewed …").
  - It is blocking: such a PR is not queued with `--auto` until lane B approves.
  - This applies to all of WP-057 and to the parts of WP-055 that handle credentials, tokens,
    webhooks signing or the MCP server.

## Rationale

The progress report of 2026-11-09 showed lane B on the critical path: about 67 sessions left,
including the discovery agent and CMDB, against about 55 for lane A and 58 for lane C. Moving
about 12 sessions evens the lanes and brings feature-complete forward. Lane B keeps its security
expertise in the loop through the blocking review.
