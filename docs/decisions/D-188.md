# D-188 (2026-11-24)

## Decision

**Access that an identity provider's group gives applies only in sessions that came through that
provider (strict).** Haider, the product owner, decided it through the coordinator, on lane B's
condition 3 for WP-057 S5c (roles and support groups from external groups).

- **Hand-made access** (a role or group membership an admin gave) applies in every session.
- **Access from an SSO group mapping** applies only when the person signed in through **this
  tenant's** provider that the mapping names. A password, LDAP or break-glass sign-in, an API key,
  a job and an act without a session (an approval by mail) do not get it. Nor does a session
  through another tenant's provider after a tenant switch.
- **Access from a directory group mapping** (Entra ID or LDAP sync) applies in every session
  except break-glass: sync removes it on its own schedule, with no sign-in needed (the
  coordinator's ruling on lane B's point (b), 2026-11-24). A directory that has not synced for
  over a day is flagged on the security overview, and its access stays as it is.
- **Routing still counts every membership:** a managed member stays in the group's queue,
  auto-assignment and notices in any session. Opening a record still enforces the session's own
  access.
- **The profile says why:** "Some of your access comes from your sign-in group and only applies
  when you sign in with <provider>", listing the roles and groups this session leaves out.
- A job a person starts runs without their SSO-group access, even when started in an SSO session,
  and its refusal says so.

## Rationale

An identity provider's group is the tenant's own statement of who belongs, and it is only fresh
when that provider's sign-in just carried it. Applying it in a password session would keep access
the provider has already removed until something else noticed. Directory sync is the exception
because it re-reads the groups on its own schedule. Later the tenant may get a setting to keep
SSO-group access on password sign-ins while the provider's last sign-in is recent.
