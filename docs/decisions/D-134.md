# D-134 (2026-11-04)

## Decision

**Possible duplicates follow the AI switch, with a non-AI fallback** (WP-038; the product owner,
through the coordinator, 2026-11-04). This replaces the overnight interim (an entitlement of its
own, separate from AI).

- Possible duplicates on the new-incident page are **gated with the AI switch**: they use
  embeddings, and "AI off" must mean all AI off.
- With AI off, they fall back to non-AI text similarity with PostgreSQL's `pg_trgm` (a contrib
  extension, approved by the product owner), available in every edition.
- Tests: AI off means no gateway call and the trigram path answers; AI on means embeddings.
- The extension is created in a migration (number from the coordinator when WP-038 starts).

## Rationale

A tenant that switches AI off expects no model to see its data. Duplicate warnings are still
useful without a model, and trigram similarity in the database needs none.

## Affected docs

PROGRESS.md (WP-038 plan), CLAUDE.md §3 (pg_trgm, a PostgreSQL contrib extension) when WP-038
lands.
