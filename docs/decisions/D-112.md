# D-112 (2026-10-28)

## Decision

**WP-020e defaults** (plan ~/Desktop/wp-020de-plan.md, approved 2026-10-28; defaults approved as listed 2026-10-28). (1) Tasks: status open / in progress / done / canceled, priority low / normal / high, owner, group, due time, at most 200 per record; "done / total" leaves canceled tasks out; order changes by moving one place up or down (no dragging), saved for everyone. Open and in-progress tasks are the record's open work for resolve and close (WP-020f port, warn by default). (2) Task-only access (REQ-INC-072 AC3) is a per-type setting, **off by default**: when on, a task's owner without access to the incident sees their open tasks (`/incidents/my-tasks`) with the incident's ID and subject and may change the task's status only; never the incident's conversation or notes. (3) Worklogs: a start is required; the end or the duration (whole minutes, 1 minute to 7 days) fills the other; both given must agree; the end is never before the start. Worklog types are a fixed list in MVP (investigation, fix, communication, travel, other); admin-editable types come with a pick list later. Owner defaults to the person logging. Times only (costs are V1, D-047). (4) Links: related (symmetrical, one link per pair) and follows (WP-020f's follow-up records, copied in from `follows_id`; the column stays until a contract step). Making a link needs edit on one record and sight of both; a reader who cannot see the other record sees that a link exists without its ID or subject, and history shows a link by its kind only. Child incidents wait for parent/child links. (5) Every task, worklog and link change is in the record's history (`child_*`, without a new record version) and refreshes open screens live.
