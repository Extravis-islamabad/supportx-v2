# Major incidents, announcements and printing

## Declaring a major incident

People with the right (by default administrators and service desk managers) can **Declare a
major incident** from an open incident's actions. A reason is required. The incident then:

- shows a red **Major** marker on its page and in every list (lists can be filtered to major
  incidents only);
- switches to the major incident template, if your administrator set one;
- tells the major incident team (a support group and named people, set under the incident
  settings). Each person is told only what they may see of the incident.

**End the major incident** works the same way, with a reason. The reason is kept on the incident
and in the audit log; the incident's history shows that the major status changed.

## Announcing from an incident

During an outage, **Announce** posts an announcement so people stop reporting the same problem.
You write the title and message (it starts from the incident's subject and number only) and
choose who sees it: everyone, chosen sites or chosen support groups, within your own scope.

If you may not publish announcements, it is saved as a draft for someone who may. The incident's
**Announcements** card lists what was announced from it, live, with **End now** (asked first).
When the incident is resolved you are offered to end them; nothing ends by itself.

## Printing or saving as PDF

**Print or save as PDF** lets you choose the sections: details, requester, conversation, tasks,
checklists, worklogs, resolution, approvals, time analysis and history. For the conversation you
choose public notes only, or all notes. The printable page shows only what you may see, in your
language and direction; use **Print**, then a printer or "Save as PDF". Attachments are not
included.
